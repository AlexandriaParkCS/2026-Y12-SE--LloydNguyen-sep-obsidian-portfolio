**Sign-Up Experience**
The Sign-Up page is a significant features for new users to access to the transaction. The design is simple and easy for any ages to access to the website.

When the user access in the website, on the right-hand side they can see the button to click immediately. As they click on the button it will take them to the Sign-Up page that they need to fill their name, choose their own username, email and the password to create an account.

**Log-In Experience**
The Log-In page is the next phase after Sign-Up page which user access to the account that they created to use the features of the website, and the information about themselves that already filled in. 

In the Home page, on the up right hand side next to Sign Up button is the Log-In button, user can immediately access to the Log-In page, or they can access through click at the line "Already got account" in Sign-Up page. In Log-In page, user requires to fill username and password to access into their account.

**Browse the gallery Experience**
As they click on the button on the home page "Browse the gallery", even they log in or not, user can see the existing posts that the artists upload with the price there. 

**Orders Experience**
When user create account as an Artist, they can see the sections "Orders" as the orders of customers will appear there.

**Post Artwork Experience**
Artists can upload the name - description - price of the artwork for the customer to choose, this will automatically appear on the homepage/gallery of the website.
