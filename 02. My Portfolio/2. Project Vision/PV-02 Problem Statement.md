The problem of new artists don't know where to start and what to do to open an art commission, find a place where they can post their commissions, promote themselves and their artworks, and don't have access to a payment method, which affects new artists who want to earn some money, start a small career, and buyers who looking for art commissions serving their own needs.

The impact of this problem is that many talented new artists struggle to enter the commission market, limiting their opportunities to earn income, build experience, and grow their creative careers. New artists get lost at the beginning and start to lose interest. This lead to the reducing of new artists, market to decline, making it difficult for buyers to place orders when they have needs that they want to be met. Existing commission platforms are complex and require too many steps without instruction, payment method limits, which are not friendly for new people to interact with.

A successful solution would be a friendly application with clearly instructions, multiple payment methods make transactions easy, page manages received orders and provides secure private messaging between the artist and the buyer. A personal page is used to briefly promote the artist's background, rights, products, and pricing. The solution focus on supporting new users, encouraging them to access and use ArtComm. This helps many new artists have the opportunity to earn their first profits and develop their skills, while making it easier for buyers to find paintings they like, discover their favourite artists, and commission custom paintings.
