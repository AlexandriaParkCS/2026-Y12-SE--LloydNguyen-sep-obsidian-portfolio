  
  
  **The user that facing the problem**
***My Friend***
* **Description:** My friend is a year 10 student, she wanted to make some income in her free time, so she can buy what she wants on her own.
* **Needs:** She needs a platform that she can upload her artworks, get commission and sell them.
* **Success Criteria:** Clear instructions, Multiple payment methods (country's bank account/master card/pay pal/...), Secure transaction, Personal profile, Manage received orders and update the process in time, Secure messaging between her (artist) and customers (who requested for commission).
### Primary Stakeholders

***Artists***
* **Description**: Independent creators who use the platforms to advertise, sell, and manage art commissions, custom artwork.
* **Needs**: Communication tools with clients, Protection from scams, Portfolio customization, Secure payment processing.
* **Success Criteria**: Increased commission requests and income, Easy workflow management, low dispute and scam rates.

***Clients/Buyers***
* **Description**: Individuals who commission artists for custom artwork, character designs.
* **Needs**: Easy artist discovery and search, Secure and trusted payment methods, Transparent pricing and timelines. 
* **Success Criteria**: Smooth commissioning experience, Secure transactions, Positive customer support experiences.

***Platform Owners***
* **Description**: The organization or individuals responsible for operating, maintaining, and growing the commission.
* **Needs**: Platform stability and scalability, Healthy community reputation, Legal and payment compliance.
* **Success Criteria**: Increased active users and transactions, High platform engagement, Positive platform reputation, Reduced operational and moderation costs.

***Moderators***
* **Description**: Staff or volunteers responsible for enforcing platform rules, resolving disputes, and maintaining community safety.
* **Needs**: Clear moderation policies, Efficient reporting and review tools, Automation tools for abuse detection.
* **Success Criteria**: Reduced scams and harassment cases, Low levels of policy violations, Safe and welcoming platform environment.


### Secondary Stakeholders

***Payment Providers***
* **Description**: Third-party financial services that process payments and payouts between buyers, artists, and the platform.
* **Needs**: Fraud prevention and chargeback management, Secure transaction systems, Compliance with financial regulations.
* **Success Criteria**: Reduced financial risk exposure, Low fraud and chargeback rates, Regulatory compliance, Stable transaction volume.

***Community / Fan Ecosystem***
* **Description**: Online fandoms, communities, collectors, and supporters that influence artist popularity and platform culture.
* **Needs**: Active and engaging community spaces, Fair moderation and inclusivity, Easy sharing and interaction features, Support for fandom and niche interests.
* **Success Criteria**: Strong community engagement, Positive platform culture, Growth of fandom participation, Frequent sharing and user interaction

***Social Media Platforms***
* **Description**: External platforms such as Discord, Reddit, Bluesky, Instagram, or Twitter/X that drive traffic and promotion to the commission website
* **Needs**: Stable integrations and authentication systems, Shareable and engaging content formats, Safe outbound linking behavior.
* **Success Criteria**: Increased referral traffic, Mutual platform growth and visibility, High user engagement through shares/posts.
