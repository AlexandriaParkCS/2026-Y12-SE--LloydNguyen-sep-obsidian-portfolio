The problem with the existing art commission PWAs is that they don't have clear instructions which can support new artists to know where to start and what to do to open an art commission, they don't know how to promote themselves and their artworks, and they don't have access to a payment method. This affects new artists who want to start their small career gave up immediately. And for those customers who are looking for art commissions to serve their own needs, they also struggle in the same way, where they are overwhelmed by a new website without any specific instructions.

The impact of this problem is many talented new artists struggling to enter the art commission market, limiting their opportunities to earn income, build experience, and grow their creative careers. This is closely related to my friend, who is also an artist looking to start her own commission. The complexity and difficulty of PWAs could wear down her patience and desire for self-improvement. It also indirectly cost her an opportunity to pursue a career aligned with her interests. Existing commission platforms are complex and require too many steps without instruction, payment method limits, which are not friendly for new people to interact with.

A successful solution would be a friendly application with clearly instructions, multiple payment methods make transactions easy, page manages received orders and provides secure private messaging between the artist and the customer. A personal page is used to briefly promote the artist's background, terms of service (TOS), products, and pricing. The solution focus on supporting new users, encouraging them to access and use ArtComm. This also encompassed all of my friend's aspirations for a PWA, helping her to successfully develop her hobby into a career.
